The Timeless Wisdom in Mottoes

When freshmen first step onto campus, they will inevitably pause in front of the stone stele bearing the college motto and take photos. And surely, these photos will soon be posted on their WeChat Moments. Besides all the likes they get, friends and family may probably ask, “What do these few words really mean?”

Indeed, a college motto is often short and concise. I can still vividly recall my first day on campus. At the welcome ceremony, our president presented the college motto to us as our first precious gift. “Seeking Truth” embodied the spirit of some pioneering scholars from our college. In the early years of the People’s Republic of China, they returned from overseas, hearts burning with passion to build a strong nation. They devoted their entire lives to seeking truth. Such role models never fail to inspire the younger generations. Our president also explained the meaning of “Persistence”, which holds the story of a group of engineering faculty members. They kept working in a modest laboratory, testing each set of data thousands of times. The mechanical parts they developed made significant contributions to our country’s space industry.

It is true that most mottoes draw on classic texts and the wisdom of sages, yet they transcend history, serving as guiding principles for our actions today and lighting the way toward our future. When we sit beside a frustrated classmate and listen patiently, we embody “Empathy”; when we listen to different voices in a debate, we practice “Inclusiveness”; when we question flawed data that supports a desired result, we uphold “Integrity.” In our future life, when we meet setbacks, the word “Mission” will help us resist the urge to “lie flat”; and “Renewal” will encourage us to keep moving forward every day.

Every such act adds warmth and meaning to these words carved in stone. So let us often reflect on the deep meaning behind these concise mottoes and put them into practice. They will guide us forward, and in doing so, we will become part of their stories.
